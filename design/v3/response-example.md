Here’s a simple release plan, organized around **three clear milestones**.

## Prepare, build, review

1. **Prepare the scope.** Agree on the tasks and what “done” means.
2. **Build the essentials.** Complete the agreed work before adding extras.
3. **Review together.** Check the result, note any gaps, and choose the next step.

## At a glance

| Milestone | Focus | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare | Align the team | Agreed scope |
| Build | Complete the essentials | Reviewable work |
| Review | Check the outcome | Next steps |

> Keep the plan small enough to review before starting the next milestone.

## A small example

A task can track its milestone with a simple `status` field:

```json
{
  "task": "Review the release",
  "status": "ready"
}
```

Use the checklist below during the review:

- [x] Scope agreed
- [ ] Review complete
