# v1 composition

A compact graphite sidebar establishes platform scope, while the light overview moves from portfolio counts to a usage chart and company issues. The company table repeats the same names, access states and usage signals found in the detail views so operators can compare and drill down.

Inter, graphite and violet come from the existing Averiq design system. The seeded layout exercise selected an asymmetric composition, a dense grid, detail disclosure and comparison rows. Its motion pass considers overlay entrance and responsive feedback. The fixed product brief favors stable reading surfaces over scroll reveals.
